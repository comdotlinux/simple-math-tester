About Simple Math Tester

Simple Math Tester is a free arithmetic practice app for school beginners, without ads, accounts or tracking. Practise addition, subtraction, multiplication and division at your own level, then review the answers. Algebra and geometry can be switched on. Progress stays on your device, and the app works offline.
