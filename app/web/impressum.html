About the app

      
Simple Math Tester is a free arithmetic practice app for children, made as a
        private, non-commercial project. It has no ads, no accounts and no tracking, and
        your progress stays on your own device.

      
    
        
  • Available on Android, iOS and the web, and works offline.
    • 
        
  • Practice addition, subtraction, multiplication and division at your own level.
